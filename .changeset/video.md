Add `@asnewyla/video`, a thin styled wrapper over the native `<video>` element — `fit`, token-backed `radius`, `aspectRatio`, and a load-error `fallback` src, with native `controls`/`autoPlay`/`loop`/`muted`/`poster`/`preload` and `<track>` children passed straight through. No custom control bar; no primitive/styled split.
