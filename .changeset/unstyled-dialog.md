Add `@asnewyla/unstyled-dialog`, an unstyled modal dialog primitive built on the native `<dialog>` element — compound components (`Dialog`/`DialogTrigger`/`DialogContent`/`DialogTitle`/`DialogDescription`/`DialogClose`), `showModal()`/`close()` lifecycle, Escape and backdrop-click dismissal, and `aria-labelledby`/`aria-describedby` wired automatically from `DialogTitle`/`DialogDescription` presence. Focus trap, background inertness, and `::backdrop` come from the browser.
