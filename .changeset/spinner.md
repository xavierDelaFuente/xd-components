Add `@asnewyla/spinner` — accessible loading indicators. `Spinner` is an indeterminate `role="status"` ring with a visually-hidden label inside the live region (`label`, `size`); `Skeleton` is a content-shaped placeholder (`width`/`height` as px-or-string, `radius`, `aria-hidden` by default). Both fall back to an opacity pulse under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Non-interactive, no primitive/styled split.
