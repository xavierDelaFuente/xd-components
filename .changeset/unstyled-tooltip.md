Add `@asnewyla/unstyled-tooltip`, an unstyled tooltip primitive — compound components (`Tooltip`/`TooltipTrigger`/`TooltipContent`), `TooltipTrigger` clones its single child, hover-open with a configurable delay, immediate open on keyboard focus, and Escape/blur/leave/press to dismiss. `aria-describedby` is wired to the content while open. CSS-only positioning via `side` → `data-side`; no portal, no collision detection.
